The Droop Quota

A critical mathematical limit. In the JHB scenario, a party must secure a minimum of 4,615 votes to be considered for its first seat allocation.

Largest Remainder Method

After primary allocation, unassigned seats are distributed to parties with the highest decimal remainders. This ensures full 270 seat occupancy.
